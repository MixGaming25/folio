## Welcome to your reading room

Folio is a small, thoughtful space for your Markdown. No accounts, no clutter. Just your files, with the room they deserve to breathe.

The familiar things, made a little nicer. **A bold idea.** *A softer thought.* A [link worth following](https://www.markdownguide.org/basic-syntax/). And a little `inline code` when words aren’t quite enough.

- Tables, task lists, and the details in between
